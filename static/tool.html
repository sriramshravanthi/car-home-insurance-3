Check your car or home insurance

Answer a few short questions, one step at a time. You get a plain-English check on your price, whether your coverage is enough, the discounts you may be missing, and a summary you can take to an agent.

This is a learning model, not a quote. Car prices start from published state medians for all 50 states and D.C. (NerdWallet, September 2026, from public rate filings) and are adjusted with factors that are sourced where noted and assumed where not. Home prices start from published state averages (Insurance.com, September 2026); another published source is 25% to 60% lower in the ten states compared, so treat home prices as rough. Home coverage checks (rebuild cost, storm deductibles, roof, stress test) use published averages where noted and our assumptions elsewhere. Legal minimums are marked as checked against an official source or taken from consumer guides. Confirm everything with your state insurance department.
