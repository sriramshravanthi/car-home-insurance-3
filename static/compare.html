About these numbers

What this is

A side-by-side of published average rates from independent research by NerdWallet and Insurance.com, for a stated sample profile. It is not a quote engine and does not pull live prices from any insurer.

What it isn't

Your actual price from any company will differ based on your exact address, claims history, credit, vehicle, and the state's approved rate filings. A company ranked "cheaper" here can still quote you higher than a company ranked "pricier."

Car insurance source

NerdWallet, published September 1, 2026. Full-coverage sample: 35-year-old, clean record, good credit, 2023 Toyota Camry LE, 12,000 miles/year. View source

Home insurance source

"Best for" categories

Not every company, every state

Not all insurers listed are licensed to sell in every state. Confirm availability and get a real quote directly from each company or a licensed agent before deciding.
