Have these ready

✓Your policy's declarations page: house limit ("dwelling" or "Coverage A"), deductibles, liability limit.
✓Your home's size, year built, roof age and material.
✓Whether you know your hurricane or wind deductible percentage, and how your roof and belongings are paid.
✓Whether you have separate flood or earthquake cover, or an umbrella policy.

Good to know

1

Standard home policies do not cover flood or earthquake, and usually not sewer backup.

2

Published rebuild costs disagree: $162 a square foot (new construction average) to $350 (a survey of actual rebuilds). Treat the tool's result as a prompt to get a professional estimate.

3

Home price data is the roughest part: another published source is 25% to 60% lower than the one used in ten states compared.

A learning tool, not a quote. Prices are estimates from published benchmarks and a model tested against one state's official insurer prices. Only a real quote shows what you would pay. Confirm anything you act on with a licensed agent and your state insurance department.
