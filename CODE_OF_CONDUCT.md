# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a small, mostly solo-maintained project. The expectation is simple: be
respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and any other discussion here.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Being considerate of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and the people using it.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussion, or bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing others' private information without their consent.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits)
and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening an issue or by contacting the maintainer
([@sriramshravanthi](https://github.com/sriramshravanthi)) directly through GitHub.
Reports will be reviewed and addressed at the maintainer's discretion, which may
include a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from the project's spaces,
depending on severity.
